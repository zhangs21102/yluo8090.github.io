Privacy Policy

BitCompressor for macOS · Effective September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how wenwenwang ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use BitCompressor (the "App").

Media files and local processing

BitCompressor accesses video files and folders that you select so it can inspect, preview, compress, and export your media. This work happens on your Mac. We do not upload your selected media or exported files to our servers. Output files are saved at the location you choose. The App may create temporary files on your Mac while processing; these are removed when the relevant operation finishes or is canceled, where possible.

Information stored on your Mac

The App stores settings locally, including language and export preferences, a selected output folder and its access bookmark, and the number of free exports used. BitCompressor uses these settings to remember your choices and apply the App's Lite and Pro features. You can change relevant preferences in the App and remove locally stored App data through macOS.

Purchases

If you choose to buy or restore Pro access, the purchase is processed through Apple's StoreKit and your App Store account. The App checks purchase status to enable Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment card information in the App. Apple's handling of purchase and account information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Feedback and support

If you use the App's feedback feature, BitCompressor opens your email app with a message addressed to bitcompressor@gmail.com. You decide whether to send it and whether to include attachments. If you send us an email, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you include. We use that information to respond to your request and provide support. Your email provider may also process that message under its own privacy policy. We retain support correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and related records. You can ask us to delete your correspondence by emailing the address above, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

The current App does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not use your App activity for cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information. We share information only when you choose to send feedback, when Apple processes an in-app purchase, or when disclosure is required by law.

Security and your choices

BitCompressor uses macOS file selection and sandbox access for the files and folders you choose. You control which media to open, where to save results, whether to buy Pro access, and whether to send feedback. To ask about information you sent to us or request its deletion, contact bitcompressor@gmail.com.

Children

BitCompressor is a general-purpose media utility and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or our data practices change. The updated version will show a new effective date on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact bitcompressor@gmail.com.
